Privacy Policy

Shabd Safar: Hindi Word Puzzle  ·  शब्द सफ़र

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game Shabd Safar: Hindi Word Puzzle (“the game”, “we”) uses. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

Developer and contact: Premkumar Mangalaraman — developerpmk@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • Your progress (the puzzle you are on, your coins, your sound setting) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and it may offer optional coin packs through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Those companies handle some data, as explained below.

संक्षेप में: खाता नहीं बनाना पड़ता। आपकी प्रगति केवल आपके फ़ोन में रहती है। खेल में Google AdMob के विज्ञापन दिखते हैं, और सिक्कों के पैक Google Play या App Store से खरीदे जा सकते हैं।

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, coins, found words and settings (such as sound and vibration) in the game’s private storage on your device. We do not receive this information. If you uninstall the game, it is deleted.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID / IDFA);
  • your IP address and approximate location (for example, your country);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, app version);
  • information about how you interact with ads.

This is used to show ads, measure how ads perform, and prevent fraud. You can control this:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Google’s own policies explain more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The game also uses Google Firebase Analytics to count, anonymously, how the game is used — for example, which level players have reached, how often the game is opened, and whether it crashes — so that we can improve it. This uses an identifier created on your device for the game (it is not your name, email or account) and general device information such as the device model, operating system, app version and approximate location (for example, your country). Where the law requires it (for example in Europe), this starts only after you agree in the consent message, and you can change your choice later using the privacy-choices button in the game. More from Google: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Purchases

The game may offer optional coin packs. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card or payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, so that we can give you the coins.

5. Country check for ads

To decide how many ads to show, the game reads the country code of your SIM card or mobile network on your device. This is used only on your device, is not stored and is not sent to us.

6. Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only parties who receive data because of the game are the services named above (Google AdMob and Google Firebase Analytics; Google Play or the Apple App Store).

8. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete or export. For data held by Google or Apple, use their account controls. You may contact us with any privacy question.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Using the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
